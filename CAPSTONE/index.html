CFO and Board Decision Brief · Fall 2026

BUS311 Individual Company Capstone

Act as an internal corporate finance analyst. Diagnose how one approved public company creates revenue and value, recommend one company-specific CFO action, and defend it to the company's CFO and Board with traceable evidence and an auditable model.

Your Board question

Given the company's revenue engine, financial position, market expectations, and risks, what specific action should the CFO recommend to the Board, and what evidence makes that decision credible and implementable?

Company-specific scope

Choose a decision the CFO can act on

Start with one direction, then rewrite it for your approved company, evidence, alternatives, and implementation path.

Capital allocation

How should the company allocate its next material dollar among organic investment, acquisitions, debt repayment, dividends, and share repurchases?

Capital structure

Should the company increase, reduce, refinance, or restructure debt?

Corporate development

Should the company pursue an acquisition, joint venture, partnership, or strategic divestiture?

Growth investment

Which major product, market, geographic, capacity, or technology investment should receive funding?

Market expectations

What operating or capital actions are required to meet or challenge the expectations embedded in the company's valuation?

Operating performance

What operating and capital actions are needed to improve ROIC, margins, or free cash flow?

Payout policy

Should the company change its dividend or share-repurchase policy?

Portfolio strategy

Should the company expand, repair, divest, or discontinue a business segment?

Risk management

Which interest-rate, foreign-exchange, commodity, refinancing, or concentration risk requires a financial response?

Working capital

How should the company improve cash conversion, inventory, receivables, payables, or liquidity?

Approval gate: submit the company and decision packet before advancing the project thesis. The instructor may narrow, redirect, or decline a scope that is descriptive, duplicative, investor-focused, or unsupported by available evidence.

Revenue, evidence, and judgment

Make every conclusion traceable

Use SEC filings and permitted FactSet evidence to diagnose the operating business, then use Excel to test what the decision could mean for value.

Revenue engine and hypothesis

  • Map products or services, segments, geographies, customers, channels, pricing, and recurring or transactional revenue.
  • Analyze three to five comparable fiscal years and reconcile reporting changes.
  • Separate company-relevant drivers such as price, volume, mix, currency, acquisitions, capacity, retention, or backlog.
  • State a testable hypothesis with a driver, expected effect, period, and falsification evidence.

Evidence register and source labels

  • Name the SEC filing, FactSet report or screen, metric, stable link, or other credible source.
  • Record the data-through or retrieval date, fiscal period, units, currency, and definition.
  • Identify the exact location and use, plus peer selection rationale and limitations when applicable.
  • Keep FactSet captures intended only for enrolled students in Canvas Course Files or another course-restricted location.

Excel milestone expectations

  • Separate inputs, sources, calculations, outputs, checks, scenarios, and sensitivities.
  • Link base, upside, and downside assumptions to the revenue hypothesis.
  • Use applicable tax, NOPAT, D&A, CapEx, working-capital, discount-rate, terminal-value, and enterprise-to-equity logic.
  • Resolve formula errors and show the assumptions most capable of changing the recommendation.

Board-ready final work

  • Use an editable 8-10-slide core PPTX with optional analytical appendix slides.
  • Create one accessible, readable, U.S.-Letter executive-summary PDF.
  • Keep decision, assumptions, results, actions, risks, limitations, and sources consistent across both files.
  • Prepare for a seven-minute briefing and three minutes of individual Q&A.

Four bounded AI red-team checkpoints

Use C-A-P-A-J: Context, Assumptions, Prompt, Assess, Judge. Free text chat is sufficient.

Checkpoint 1

Question generation

Generate possible CFO decisions and research directions after company selection.

Checkpoint 2

Revenue-hypothesis challenge

Ask a skeptical CFO to identify weak assumptions, alternatives, missing variables, and falsification evidence.

Checkpoint 3

Assumption audit

Inspect a student-written sanitized assumption list for omissions, inconsistency, and scenario risk.

Checkpoint 4

Board Q&A rehearsal

Rehearse questions on revenue, valuation, implementation, and risk using a student-written summary.

AI is not a source. Verify every retained claim or calculation with SEC, FactSet, Excel, or course concepts. Record whether you accept, modify, or reject the challenge and why. Do not upload raw licensed FactSet data, screenshots, private information, or instructor-only materials to public AI tools.

Frequently asked questions

Know the boundaries

These answers summarize the approved project contract. Canvas controls any later instructor-announced change.

Is this a group project?

No. Research, evidence selection, modeling, writing, slides, delivery, and Q&A are individual work.

Is a Buy, Hold, or Sell recommendation enough?

No. Market valuation may support the analysis, but the project requires one company-specific action the CFO could recommend to the Board.

Do I need a paid AI plan?

No. Each checkpoint is designed for free text chat or an instructor-provided noncommercial equivalent. Browsing, uploads, connectors, image generation, and spreadsheet analysis are not required.

Can I upload FactSet files or screenshots to a public AI tool?

No. Keep raw FactSet exports and screenshots in Canvas Course Files or another course-restricted location. Use only sanitized, student-written summaries in public AI tools.

Is AI output a source?

No. Verify retained claims with SEC filings, permitted FactSet evidence, Excel calculations, or course concepts, then record an accept, modify, or reject judgment.

What happens to the Excel model at the end?

The working Excel model is submitted and assessed at Milestone 3. It is not a third final communication file unless the instructor requests it for Q&A verification.

What are the final communication files?

Submit exactly two files: the editable PPTX Board brief and the one-page executive summary PDF.

Which dates and submission links control if something changes?

Canvas is the authority for live submission links, exact due times, presentation order, and any later instructor-announced change.

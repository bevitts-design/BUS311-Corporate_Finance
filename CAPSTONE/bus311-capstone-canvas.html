CFO decision suggestion menu

Choose a direction, rewrite it for your approved company, and submit the approval packet before advancing the thesis.

Capital allocation

How should the company allocate its next material dollar among organic investment, acquisitions, debt repayment, dividends, and share repurchases?

Capital structure

Should the company increase, reduce, refinance, or restructure debt?

Corporate development

Should the company pursue an acquisition, joint venture, partnership, or strategic divestiture?

Growth investment

Which major product, market, geographic, capacity, or technology investment should receive funding?

Market expectations

What operating or capital actions are required to meet or challenge the expectations embedded in the company's valuation?

Operating performance

What operating and capital actions are needed to improve ROIC, margins, or free cash flow?

Payout policy

Should the company change its dividend or share-repurchase policy?

Portfolio strategy

Should the company expand, repair, divest, or discontinue a business segment?

Risk management

Which interest-rate, foreign-exchange, commodity, refinancing, or concentration risk requires a financial response?

Working capital

How should the company improve cash conversion, inventory, receivables, payables, or liquidity?

Approval gate: the instructor may narrow, redirect, or decline a scope that is descriptive, duplicative, investor-focused, or unsupported by available evidence.

Required analytical spine

Revenue engine and hypothesis

Map how the company earns revenue, analyze three to five comparable years, identify company-specific drivers and risks, and state a testable hypothesis with falsification evidence.

SEC, FactSet, and evidence register

Trace every material claim and assumption. Record source, date, period, units, definition, exact location and use, peer rationale, and limitations.

Excel valuation milestone

Separate inputs, sources, calculations, outputs, checks, scenarios, and sensitivities. Connect base, upside, and downside cases to the revenue hypothesis.

Board-ready communication

Keep the decision, assumptions, results, actions, risks, limitations, source labels, and AI disclosure consistent across the PPTX and one-page PDF.

Four bounded AI red-team checkpoints

Use C-A-P-A-J: Context, Assumptions, Prompt, Assess, Judge. Free text chat is sufficient; paid features are not required.

Checkpoint 1: Question generation

Generate possible CFO decisions and research directions after company selection.

Checkpoint 2: Revenue-hypothesis challenge

Ask a skeptical CFO to identify weak assumptions, alternatives, missing variables, and falsification evidence.

Checkpoint 3: Assumption audit

Inspect a student-written sanitized assumption list for omissions, inconsistency, and scenario risk.

Checkpoint 4: Board Q&A rehearsal

Rehearse questions on revenue, valuation, implementation, and risk using a student-written summary.

AI is not a source. Independently verify retained claims with SEC, FactSet, Excel, or course concepts, then record accept, modify, or reject judgment. Never upload raw FactSet exports or screenshots, student data, or instructor-only material to public AI tools.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a group project?

No. Research, evidence selection, modeling, writing, slides, delivery, and Q&A are individual work.

Is a Buy, Hold, or Sell recommendation enough?

No. Market valuation may support the analysis, but the project requires one company-specific action the CFO could recommend to the Board.

Do I need a paid AI plan?

No. Each checkpoint is designed for free text chat or an instructor-provided noncommercial equivalent. Browsing, uploads, connectors, image generation, and spreadsheet analysis are not required.

Can I upload FactSet files or screenshots to a public AI tool?

No. Keep raw FactSet exports and screenshots in Canvas Course Files or another course-restricted location. Use only sanitized, student-written summaries in public AI tools.

Is AI output a source?

No. Verify retained claims with SEC filings, permitted FactSet evidence, Excel calculations, or course concepts, then record an accept, modify, or reject judgment.

What happens to the Excel model at the end?

The working Excel model is submitted and assessed at Milestone 3. It is not a third final communication file unless the instructor requests it for Q&A verification.

What are the final communication files?

Submit exactly two files: the editable PPTX Board brief and the one-page executive summary PDF.

Which dates and submission links control if something changes?

Canvas is the authority for live submission links, exact due times, presentation order, and any later instructor-announced change.

Student progress checklist

  1. I am completing the project individually for one instructor-approved U.S. public company.
  2. My question asks the CFO and Board to make one specific, material company decision.
  3. I compared credible alternatives and received approval before advancing the thesis.
  4. My evidence register identifies each SEC filing, FactSet item, period, units, definition, location, use, and limitation.
  5. My revenue map explains products or services, segments, geographies, customers, channels, pricing, and recurring or transactional revenue where relevant.
  6. My three-to-five-year revenue history uses comparable periods and reconciles reporting changes.
  7. My testable hypothesis names the driver, expected effect, period, and evidence that could support or falsify it.
  8. My base, upside, and downside cases flow from the revenue diagnosis.
  9. My Excel model separates inputs, sources, calculations, outputs, checks, scenarios, and sensitivities.
  10. I completed all four C-A-P-A-J checkpoints using free text chat or the instructor-provided equivalent.

  12. My one-page executive summary and PPTX state the same decision, assumptions, results, actions, risks, and source labels.
  13. My core deck contains eight to ten editable slides; appendix slides are reserved for Q&A support.
  14. I can deliver the briefing in seven minutes and answer questions for three minutes without relying on AI.

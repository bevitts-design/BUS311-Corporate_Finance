Planned final presentation

Six core slides plus optional supporting appendix. Final PowerPoint: 50 points. Live presentation: 25 points, up to seven minutes plus three minutes of questions. Keep the existing final deadline of Monday, Nov. 30, 2026 at 12:30 p.m. ET, when class begins unless Canvas announces a change.

  1. Company and revenue engine. Explain who pays, what they buy, and how the company charges. Revenue map and largest disclosed revenue source.
  2. Revenue history and drivers. Explain the recent sales trend and its causes. Three-year revenue chart and two supported drivers.
  3. Proposed growth opportunity. Explain the customer, willingness to pay, and company fit. Simple revenue estimate with labeled assumptions and displaced sales.
  4. Financial comparison. Explain what company trends and peer ratios imply for the proposal. Financial strength, constraint, and effect on the proposed action.
  5. Brief P/E check. Interpret the peer-based estimate and its limitations. Consistent EPS and share-price dates, selected multiple, and a small illustrative range.
  6. Recommendation and next step. Recommend pursue, pilot, or strengthen first. Explain risk and success measure. One practical next action and an observable reconsideration trigger.

Sources, individual work, and revisions

Research, assumptions, formulas, writing, and presentation are individual work. You must explain the submitted analysis yourself.

Use credible company filings, annual reports, or FactSet reports to support company facts and historical figures. Name the relevant filing or report and useful page, section, or link in your written responses. Clearly label calculations, assumptions, and interpretations. AI output is not financial evidence.

Students and the instructor may use FactSet evidence in BUS311 course deliverables. Raw exports and screenshots remain in approved course-restricted systems and are not committed publicly or uploaded to public AI tools.

Give public AI tools only a short summary you wrote from public information. Do not upload workbooks, raw FactSet exports or screenshots, personal information, private teaching material, or nonpublic information. If AI is used, name the tool and purpose in the workbook revision response and disclose it briefly in the presentation appendix or source notes.

Use the required minimum where evidence supports it. If no defensible peer or comparable historical figure exists, state why and discuss an appropriate limitation or proxy with the instructor. Never invent company facts. Label estimated project inputs as assumptions and explain their basis.

Milestones remain eligible under the BUS311 syllabus late-work policy. A late milestone delays feedback and can make dependent project work harder; complete missing milestone work before relying on it in a later stage. Documented exceptions follow the applicable college and course process.

Milestones 1–3 may each be revised once within seven calendar days of written feedback. Replace the workbook in the original assignment; the higher score replaces the original. For Milestone 2, summarize changes in Next dollar; for Milestone 3, summarize revisions in AI challenge.

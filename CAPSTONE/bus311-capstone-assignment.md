# BUS311 Individual Company Capstone

Explain how your company earns revenue, evaluate a growth opportunity, and use financial comparisons to recommend its next move.

The Follow the Dollar workbook replaces the earlier Revenue/Model workflow for Milestone 2. Prior NPV, full valuation, and cash-flow-model requirements are superseded in this revised project. Keep your Milestone 1 work.

Milestone 2 and Milestone 3 materials are complete. The optional final presentation template remains a separate follow-up.

Existing course milestone dates are retained as schedule references. Check Canvas for the applicable submission deadline and any instructor-announced change.

## Stage 1: Choose a company and decision

5 points. Existing schedule reference: September 9 at 12:30 p.m. ET.

Complete Start: company, ticker, a short company snapshot, one FactSet learning and feature used, and one tentative decision.

Submit: Upload the continuing workbook with Start complete.

## Stage 2: Follow the Dollar—Find the Next Dollar

8 points. Existing schedule reference: September 30 at 12:30 p.m. ET.

Trace the company’s current revenue engine, explain recent sales, and develop one evidence-supported revenue opportunity that you can evaluate financially in Milestone 3.

Submit: Submit one completed .xlsx workbook with all three tabs. Use your approved project company. No separate report, slide deck, peer-ratio analysis, or full valuation is required for Milestone 2.

Grading: Revenue engine 2; history and drivers 2; growth opportunity and estimate 2; feasibility, challenge, and revision 2.

## Stage 3: Compare, Evaluate, and Recommend

12 points. Existing schedule reference: November 4 at 12:30 p.m. ET.

Evaluate how effectively your company turns revenue into financial performance. Compare two defensible peers, use a brief P/E estimate for market context, and revise your growth recommendation.

Submit: Submit one completed .xlsx workbook with Recommendation, Company ratios, Peer comparison, and P-E check complete. Keep the same approved company and bring forward the chosen Milestone 2 opportunity. No separate report or full valuation model is required.

Grading: Peer selection and inputs 3; ratios and interpretation 3; P/E check 2; recommendation and revision 4.

## Stage 4: Recommend and present

75 points. Existing schedule reference: PowerPoint: November 30 at 12:30 p.m. ET; presentations: November 30–December 9.

Use six core slides to explain your decision, evidence, numbers, risks, and next step. Put supporting calculations and sources in an optional appendix.

Submit: Upload one editable PowerPoint, then present that file live. Up to 7 minutes presenting and 3 minutes answering questions.

Grading: PowerPoint 50; Oral Presentation 25.

## Milestone 3

- Use three comparable company fiscal years and the latest reasonably comparable completed year for each peer. Show exact year ends. Use the same accounting/ratio definitions and appropriate average balances. Name the actual selected-peer median, not an industry average. Two peers are a small descriptive sample.
- Calculate and interpret five appropriate ratios and one comparison chart.
- Complete the brief peer-based P/E range using consistent positive trailing earnings.
- Revise the Milestone 2 opportunity using financial strengths, constraints, a checked challenge, and a measurable next step.

A full company valuation or NPV project model is not required. The P/E check offers market context; it does not measure the value created by the proposed initiative.

Beta describes sensitivity on a continuum

Interpretation
β < 0Tends to move opposite the market
β = 0No fitted market sensitivity
0 < β < 1Moves less than the market
β = 1Market-like sensitivity
β > 1Amplifies market movement

Beta is dimensionless and time-varying. Always document the benchmark, frequency, and estimation window.

## Factual, rights, and privacy disposition

- The PowerPoint, repeated template art, extracted media, rendered references, and proprietary FactSet screens remain outside the public repository.
- Apple FY2024 figures come from Apple’s consolidated financial statements: common stock and APIC $83.276B, accumulated deficit $(19.154)B, accumulated other comprehensive loss $(7.172)B, and total equity $56.950B.
- The source’s separate Apple APIC and treasury-stock construction is not carried forward because it does not match Apple’s reported FY2024 balance-sheet presentation and would not reconcile arithmetically.
- Apple FY2024 net income was $93.736B; dividends and common-stock repurchases totaled $110.183B. The deck uses this as a directional explanation, not as a complete roll-forward of accumulated deficit.
- Alphabet’s 2025 proxy confirms one vote for Class A, ten votes for Class B, and the shareholder statement that Page and Brin controlled more than 51% of voting power while owning less than 13% of stock.
- Alphabet’s Q2 2026 Form 10-Q supports the new FactSet exercise and answer reveal: $30.499B of net common-stock proceeds, $19.063B of net mandatory-convertible-preferred proceeds, 114M common shares issued during Q2, and no shares sold under the $40B ATM program by June 30.
- The Q2 equity statement supplies the matched March 31 and June 30 share, common-stock/APIC, and total-equity balances. The balance sheet supplies the December 31 and June 30 cash, marketable-securities, long-term-debt, and total-equity balances. The deck labels these different comparison windows rather than implying they are identical periods.
- The GOOGL event window uses the unadjusted May 29 and June 5, 2026 closes, $380.34 and $368.53. The exercise requires students to record the FactSet price field and date window because adjusted settings can produce small presentation differences.
- No proprietary FactSet screenshot, export, or licensed data file is stored in the repository. The public deck contains an original workflow mockup and a limited instructor-verified answer table; students perform the live retrieval inside their licensed FactSet sessions.
- IRS Publication 542 states that eligible domestic-corporation dividends may receive a 50% deduction below 20% ownership and 65% at 20% or more, subject to limits and holding-period rules.
- Delta’s 2025 Form 10-K supplies the current capstone evidence and explicitly states that expected 2026 capital spending may vary depending on financing decisions.
- The historical funding percentages and categorical preferred-yield conclusion are retained in this inventory but not projected because they are undated approximations and too absolute for a decision-ready deck.

## Teaching throughline

Students move from auditable public evidence to a valuation recommendation. They first choose a model that matches the claim, build a one-period and Gordon-growth valuation in visible Excel-style cells, test sensitivity before seeing the answer, extend the reasoning to a multi-stage model, and finish with a decision standard that explicitly connects evidence, calculation, assumption, and recommendation.

## Design and activity decisions

- Use the approved BUS311 navy, steel, teal, gold, and terra visual system.
- Use editable HTML/CSS/SVG for the evidence workflow, decision tree, valuation bridges, sensitivity matrix, IPO process, and recommendation chain.
- Preserve the strong editable valuation and Excel visuals from the prior lesson while consolidating repetitive concept slides.
- Use a dedicated 21-slide source instead of the generic lesson generator.
- Provide explicit setup, individual attempt, peer check, reveal, timing, response method, and deliverable where an activity occurs.
- Keep formative answers, rationale, misconceptions, and debrief prompts in substantive teaching notes.
- Use a self-contained deck runtime with no external JavaScript and no separate Excel activity workbook.

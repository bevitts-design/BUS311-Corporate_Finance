A note from Professor Evitts

Welcome—I’m glad you’re here.

I’m Bethany Evitts, and I will be your professor for BUS311. With more than three decades in investment and financial management, I have seen financial markets through many cycles and watched how strong decisions create lasting value.

In this course, we will move beyond memorizing formulas. You will use financial statements, Excel models, market data, and valuation tools to analyze real choices, communicate your reasoning, and recommend a course of action.

Come ready to work

Bring your fully charged laptop to every class.

We will regularly build Excel models, work with FactSet, and interpret the results together.

The course journey

Each part of BUS311 adds another layer to one central question: How do financial decisions create value?

01   Foundations

Read the business

Understand markets, financial institutions, statements, ratios, ethics, and the flow of capital.

02   Valuation

Value the cash flows

Apply time value of money, bond and equity valuation, risk and return, and the cost of capital.

03   Decisions

Choose and defend

Evaluate investments, financing choices, capital structure, and the trade-offs behind a recommendation.
